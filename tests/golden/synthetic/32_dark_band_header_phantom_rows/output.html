o Net Zero & Climate Risk Working Forum ("NZCRWF"): A global quarterly bimonthly
forum/working group with representation from key stakeholders across Client Coverage, Chief Sustainability Officer (CSO) Team, Credit Officers/Industries Risk, ESGR Client Risk Management team was established under the purview of the CIB Client Review Committee ("CRC") in 2023.
