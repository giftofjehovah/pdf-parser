Vertically Merged Cells (Invisible Row Separators)
The first column visually merges three rows because the inner row dividers are drawn in the page background colour. A reader sees one merged cell containing three lines of text; the PDF data, however, still encodes three separate rows split by white-on-white lines. A colour-aware parser must honour the visual reading.
