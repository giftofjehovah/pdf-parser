o Long Term Strategic Planning Workgroup ("LTSPW"): A periodic recurring meeting / standing group with participation drawn from across various functions including Resource Planning, Quality Assurance Office (QAO) Team, Internal Reviewers, Subject-Matter/Discipline Experts, and the Operational Risk Management department has been formally constituted under the supervision of the Central Review Board ("CRB") in the prior planning cycle. The purpose of the group is to review, validate, approve and track progress across all initiatives that have been flagged as high priority and/or are not aligned with the agreed delivery roadmap of the programme.
